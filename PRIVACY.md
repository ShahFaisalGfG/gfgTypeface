# Privacy Policy

gfg Typeface changes fonts, text size, line spacing and weight on the web pages you visit.

## What is stored

- Your all-sites defaults: chosen fonts, sizes, spacing, weight and language fonts.
- For each site you give its own settings: the site's host name (for example `example.com`) and those settings.

These settings are stored in your browser's `storage.sync` area. If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser syncs them between your devices through your browser account.

The update check keeps its state on this device only, in the browser's `storage.local` area: whether the check is on, the latest release it found, when it last checked and the version whose notice you dismissed.

## What is not collected

gfg Typeface does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Page content is read only inside your browser, to apply your settings, and never leaves it.

The one request gfg Typeface sends is the update check: once a day, a copy loaded unpacked asks `api.github.com` whether a newer release is out. The request is anonymous. It carries no settings, no sites you visit and no cookies, and GitHub sees only what any web request shows, such as your IP address and browser version. To turn it off, open **All settings** and switch off **Check for updates once a day**.

## Your control

Open **All settings** to see every stored site, export your settings to a file, remove individual sites or reset everything. Removing the extension deletes its settings.
