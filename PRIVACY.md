# Privacy Policy

gfg Typeface changes fonts, text size, line spacing and weight on the web pages you visit.

## What is stored

- Your all-sites defaults: chosen fonts, sizes, spacing, weight and language fonts.
- For each site you give its own settings: the site's host name (for example `example.com`) and those settings.

These settings are stored in your browser's `storage.sync` area. If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser syncs them between your devices through your browser account.

## What is not collected

gfg Typeface does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Page content is read only inside your browser, to apply your settings, and never leaves it.

## Your control

Open **All settings** to see every stored site, export your settings to a file, remove individual sites or reset everything. Removing the extension deletes its settings.
